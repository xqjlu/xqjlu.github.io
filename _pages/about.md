I am a Professor of Physics at [Jilin University](https://www.jlu.edu.cn/). My research focuses on deriving, developing, and implementing efficient electronic-structure methods for large-scale condensed-matter simulations. I am one of the main developers of the VG-rtTDDFTB, ATLAS, and ARES software packages.

Email: xuq(at)jlu.edu.cn

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on novel theoretical and computational methods for mesoscale condensed-matter physics:
* Orbital-free density functional theory ([OFDFT](https://doi.org/10.1002/wcms.1724));
* Velocity-gauge real-time time-dependent density functional tight-binding (VG-rtTDDFTB);
* Real-space pseudopotential electronic-structure methods (ARES);
* Theoretical modeling of condensed-matter phenomena.

[Explore my research →](/research/)

Developed Packages
======
* **VG-rtTDDFTB:** velocity-gauge real-time time-dependent density functional tight-binding for electron dynamics in systems with thousands of atoms.
* **ARES:** an *ab initio* real-space electronic-structure package for systems containing up to tens of thousands of atoms.
* **[ATLAS](http://atlas-ch.cn/):** orbital-free density functional theory software for simulations ranging from thousands to millions of atoms.
