## VG-rtTDDFTB

**Velocity-Gauge Real-Time Time-Dependent Density Functional Tight-Binding** enables electron-dynamics simulations of large periodic systems. The method is implemented in DFTB+; the research implementation is not currently distributed as a separate package.

Relevant publications:

1. [Q. Xu et al., *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c12952)
2. [Q. Xu et al., *J. Chem. Theory Comput.* (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00689)

## ARES

**Ab Initio Real-Space Electronic Structures** is an in-house Kohn–Sham DFT package for large-scale calculations with periodic and non-periodic boundary conditions. The code is currently available through research collaboration rather than as a public download.

Relevant publication:

1. [Q. Xu et al., *J. Phys.: Condens. Matter* (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-648X/ab2a63)

## ATLAS

**Ab Initio Orbital-Free Density Functional Theory Software** supports large-scale material simulations. Visit the [ATLAS website](http://atlas-ch.cn/) for downloads and further information.

Relevant publications:

1. [Q. Xu et al., *Nat. Commun.* (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29002-3)
2. [Q. Xu et al., *Phys. Rev. B* (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.101.045110)
3. [Q. Xu et al., *Phys. Rev. B* (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.100.205132)
4. [X. Shao et al., *Comput. Phys. Commun.* (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2018.07.009)
5. [W. Mi et al., *Comput. Phys. Commun.* (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2015.11.004)
